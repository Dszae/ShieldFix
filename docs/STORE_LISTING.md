# Store listing (draft)

**Name:** ShieldFix

**Short description (≤ 132 characters):**
Detects anti-adblock warnings and offers reversible, user-approved fixes. Private by design: everything stays on your device.

**Category:** Privacy & Security

**Detailed description:**

Websites increasingly react to ad and tracker blocking with full-screen “please disable your ad blocker” walls, banners
and broken players. ShieldFix helps you keep your protection on.

- Recognises anti-adblock warnings and tells them apart from cookie banners, sign-in dialogs, subscription prompts and
  ordinary pop-ups.
- Explains what it found and why, with a clear confidence level.
- Offers the least invasive fix: hide the warning, restore scrolling and remove blur — only when you approve it, and
  always reversible with one click. Ads and trackers stay blocked.
- Remembers fixes for sites you choose, with rules you can review, edit, roll back, export or delete.
- Explains how to adjust Brave Shields for a single site when a cosmetic fix is not enough. ShieldFix never changes your
  protection itself.
- Optional automatic detection for the sites you pick.

Privacy: no data collection, no analytics, no network requests, no account. Everything runs and stays on your device.

ShieldFix does not bypass paywalls, logins or subscription requirements.
